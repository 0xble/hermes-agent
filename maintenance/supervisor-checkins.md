# Supervisor Check-ins

## Preserve

Automatically supervise gateway-owned background work without occupying a user loop or heartbeat slot. Keep the state on the compression lineage, deliver to the proven original topic/profile/bot, and never wake a new or forked conversation for its predecessor's work. Only acknowledged visible output resets the cadence. Execution start, interruption and failed, held or deferred sends retain the overdue deadline and visible anchor. A separate persisted execution fence permits at most 1 fresh model attempt per recurring interval while the update remains overdue. Native admission still excludes live or pending turns, and the existing bounded delivery ledger/outbox owns retries of produced output, not another model loop. No outstanding work means no check-in turn or status message. LOCAL routes retain their registered source capability, and their visible-output acknowledgment is the successfully committed, replayable final result, not the local adapter's no-op send.

The `supervisor_checkin` display kind is generated machinery with a mandatory written status. It is the bounded exception to unchanged-wait silence, not permission to make ordinary goal or loop wakeups chatty. Reaction-only acknowledgement does not satisfy it. The existing one-shot gateway silence continuation owns model recovery, and normal final delivery owns the factual fallback, never a second transport send path. A failed or partial model turn, including a nonempty provider-error explainer, must yield a fresh factual pending status after generic response shaping. Preserve the failure verdict while replacing the delivered text and captured result together. Refresh the roster before sending any status, and suppress the reply and disarm if the work has finished. Supervisor turns suppress intermediate engine status and diagnostic chrome, so a provider-error acknowledgment cannot reset cadence before the roster-validated final. Real user interruptions and queued human successors retain priority.

A user's queued message, completion, existing machinery input, `/stop`, emergency stop, and runtime draining take priority. Validate work and the due identity again at execution, not only when scanning. Observation failures retain the candidate and log the failure, rather than converting unknown state to completion. Reconcile external effects before recovery or restart.

## Update And Retirement

Compare upstream idle admission and task supervision before porting this patch. Adopt an upstream implementation only when it covers automatic arming, durable timer state, execution-time cancellation, original topic routing, mandatory bounded written updates, and completion disarming together. A heartbeat or progress notifier alone is not a replacement. Explicit peer dependencies live on the existing goal snapshot, with the existing CAS, current-session context, recorded-evidence validation, and revision history. Do not introduce an external task ledger or infer dependencies from arbitrary relay traffic. Preserve pending references across ordinary barrier, route, restart, and compression changes. Judge and autonomous completion must both refuse unresolved work. An armed local-roster error retains the due clock and takes the normal unknown-status path when due, never a remote heartbeat probe.

Its peer-only case registers through the current-session CLI and native RPC, verifies due admission and a written original-topic status, refuses an unrecorded result, and proves explicit user-clear disarming. The unit and owner-RPC cases cover exact evidence resolution, both completion routes, CAS conflicts, multiple peers, native controls, compression/restart, unknown-roster due handling, and unarmed no-work silence. The ordered-admission and execution-entry daemon barriers verify human-input priority and continued FIFO drain. The LOCAL daemon proof kills separate owners after queued commit and during model execution, verifies durable descriptor restoration, interrupted-attempt fencing, one silence recovery, status persistence and replay without duplicate work. Accelerating a persisted due timestamp in this fixture does not prove a natural-cadence live peer round trip. Use the repository exact-head gate before protected landing. Disable `checkins.enabled` or revert this unit to remove automatic supervision without altering existing loops, goals or completion delivery.
