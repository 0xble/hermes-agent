# Supervisor Check-ins

## Preserve

Automatically supervise gateway-owned background work without occupying a user loop or heartbeat slot. Keep the state on the compression lineage, deliver to the proven original topic/profile/bot, and never wake a new or forked conversation for its predecessor's work. Only acknowledged visible output resets the cadence. Execution start, interruption and failed, held or deferred sends retain the overdue deadline and visible anchor. A separate persisted execution fence permits at most 1 fresh model attempt per recurring interval while the update remains overdue. Native admission still excludes live or pending turns, and the existing bounded delivery ledger/outbox owns retries of produced output, not another model loop. No outstanding work means no check-in turn or status message. LOCAL routes retain their registered source capability, and their visible-output acknowledgment is the successfully committed, replayable final result, not the local adapter's no-op send.

The `supervisor_checkin` display kind is generated machinery. Inspect and steer work within existing authority, then report material progress, consequential changed risk, actionable stalls or decisions, and verified completion. Preserve useful initial reassurance for genuine active work. After a successful inspection, an explicit `NO_REPLY` or `[SILENT]` may stay quiet for unchanged, understood future or dependency waits. Age alone and a surviving dependency never require a message or another next-check promise. Quiet inspection retains unresolved work and the persisted execution fence, without advancing `last_visible_at`, creating a delivery acknowledgment, resolving peers or completing goals. It still settles the turn and its durable replay result normally.

A failed, partial, absent or unknown inspection is not deliberate quiet. Refresh the roster and use normal final delivery for factual pending or unknown status when needed, never a second transport send path. Preserve the failure verdict while normalizing the delivered text and captured result together. Suppress generated fallback and disarm only when a fresh roster confirms no work remains. Preserve a valid model-written status or final result, including when it verifies and clears the last dependency. Supervisor markers no longer trigger the human-turn silence continuation. Reaction-only acknowledgment or empty output cannot establish a successful quiet inspection. Hooks and the failed-turn transcript boundary use the normalized pending status, not the provider explainer.

Supervisor turns suppress intermediate engine status, diagnostic chrome, interim commentary/streaming, progress sends and edits, subagent-failure notices and inactivity warnings. The inactivity timeout and host-sleep accounting remain active. Real user interruptions and queued human successors retain priority. Silence suppresses delivery, not transcript closure, hooks or resume-marker settlement. Failed turns retain their failure verdict.

A user's queued message, completion, existing machinery input, `/stop`, emergency stop, and runtime draining take priority. Validate work and the due identity again at execution, not only when scanning. Observation failures retain the candidate and log the failure, rather than converting unknown state to completion. Reconcile external effects before recovery or restart.

## Provenance And Patches

- **Identity:** `supervisor-checkins`, active fork feature.
- **Baseline:** maintained fork `origin/main`, `1d587b5821e6ad9b49158a1a70886db757a3b089`.
- **Why Core:** idle gateway turn completion, native automation admission, structured provenance, and final delivery policy are core boundaries. An opt-in plugin timer cannot safely guarantee this contract for every owned delegation/process.
- **Prior Art:** closed, unmerged upstream [#22052](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/pull/22052) proposed a gateway check-in scanner but deferred live prompt injection. The feature retains completion push as the fast path and adds bounded supervision as fallback. It does not replace the active-turn notification interval.
- **Quiet-wait correction:** The original fork commit `b84751aa749dbce59e9515e1bb025057bd9d9dc5` required every-tick visibility. The bounded correction retains inspection and cadence while allowing successful unchanged waits to stay quiet. Upstream heartbeat guidance at `2281116525106e3c109993ccb5dc74d02bfc1427` separates inspection from meaningful-change delivery. It supplies policy guidance, not a replacement supervisor implementation.
- **Related Units:** [Internal notification silence](internal-notification-silence.md), [Gateway silence re-prompt](gateway-silence-reprompt.md), [Native admission metadata](native-admission-synthetic-metadata.md), and [Gateway state DB guard](gateway-loop-statedb-guard.md).
- **Delivery:** commits carrying `Fork-Patch: supervisor-checkins`. Protected fork landing requires exact-head qualification and independent review. Source delivery does not activate Hermes.

## Update And Retirement

Compare upstream idle admission and task supervision before porting this patch. Adopt an upstream implementation only when it covers automatic arming, durable timer state, execution-time cancellation, original topic routing, material-change reporting with successful quiet waits, and completion disarming together. A heartbeat or progress notifier alone is not a replacement. Explicit peer dependencies live on the existing goal snapshot, with the existing CAS, current-session context, recorded-evidence validation, and revision history. Do not introduce an external task ledger or infer dependencies from arbitrary relay traffic. Preserve pending references across ordinary barrier, route, restart, and compression changes. Judge and autonomous completion must both refuse unresolved work. An armed local-roster error retains the due clock and takes the normal unknown-status path when due, never a remote heartbeat probe.
